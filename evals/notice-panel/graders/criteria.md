A successful response meets all of these:

1. The panel shows only the latest few notifications (about ten), with a link/button to the full list ("Show all"), plus "Mark all as read".
2. Deadlines or time-critical items are placed at the top and marked with a text label, and unread state is shown with text (tag or label), not by colour or bold alone.
3. Each row names subject, sender/source and time and leads to its target; opening an entry marks it read and closes the panel.
4. On open, focus moves into the panel (for example to its heading); Escape closes it and focus returns to the bell button.
5. The panel does not navigate away from the current work, and it is usable on narrow screens (for example full width).

Score 1 only if at least four of the five points are clearly present and none is contradicted.
