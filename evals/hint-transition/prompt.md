Our pages have an information "(i)" button in the header that collects the hints a person has
dismissed on that page. When someone clicks the "×" on a hint today, the hint just vanishes and
keyboard focus jumps to the top of the document. Testers do not realise the hint went into the
(i), and screen-reader users lose their place.

Design what should happen at the moment a hint is dismissed. Give numbered acceptance criteria,
including accessibility.
