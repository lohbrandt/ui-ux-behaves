In our web app people can dismiss the info hints at the top of a page with an "×". Several
colleagues complain: "I closed the tip about bulk export last week and now I can't find it any
more." The product owner suggests putting dismissed hints into the notification bell in the
header, next to the error messages and the "your session expires" warning, so nothing is lost.

Is that a good idea? Propose where dismissed hints should go instead and write numbered
acceptance criteria for it.
